Shipping the parser fix does not close #4471. The hint and one-day reports keep coming after it ships, because their causes are elsewhere.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so any date whose day is 12 or less comes out with day and month swapped: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names. Its fix is the one queued.

hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong format under its date field. Tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The dates swap the same way, but the parser fix does not touch them. Only reports sent through the form of a day-first tenant on booking-v3 belong here. The fix is the hint (or the form), not the parser.

one-day: dates move by one day, not by a swap. These reports have no day/month swap in them. A Tuesday shows as the Monday before it, a whole month is “one day off”, one client gets Tuesday and another gets Monday for the same meeting, an invite arrives before the booking was made. That pattern points at time zones (a date stored or rendered at midnight in the wrong zone), not at date order. The desk saw it and wrote these ids down in desk-notes.json, but #4471 still counts them under parser.

How the reports are placed

  1. Count each report once. The export repeats some reports (pages overlap); one id is one report.
  2. Place by tenant, not by intake tag. meta.intakeTag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form, and on the channel (causeOf in causes.ts). Intake’s tags disagree with that for dozens of reports, in both directions; the table shows the split.
  3. A one-day move is its own problem. A report listed in desk-notes.json as a one-day move goes to one-day before the tenant rule runs (problemOf in problems.ts).

Every number on this page is computed from reports-4471.json when the page renders. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, the counts here move with them.

Why one ticket hid three

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone. All three problems produce the same subjects (“Wrong day on the invite”, “Month and day swapped?”), so the subject rule cannot tell them apart.
